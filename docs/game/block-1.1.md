# Block 1.1 playtest guide

Preview: https://s-tian86.github.io/openttd-web/preview/block-1-1/

Open it on your phone. Everything here is meant to be tried with your fingers.

## What is new

- An empty, flat map: a green grid with a dark border at the edges.
- A calendar starting on 1 January 1950, with £100,000 in the bank.
- Pause and four speeds: normal, ×2, ×4 and fast forward (25×, like OpenTTD).
- Settings: language (English or Español) and theme (only "Blocks" for now).

## What to try on the phone

- [ ] Drag with one finger to move the map.
- [ ] Pinch to zoom in and out. The page itself must not zoom.
- [ ] Find the edge of the map.
- [ ] Watch the date advance (one game day is about 2 seconds at normal speed).
- [ ] Pause and check that the date stops.
- [ ] Try each speed.
- [ ] Switch to Español and back to English.
- [ ] Rotate the phone.
- [ ] Pinch on the bottom bar. The page must not zoom.

## Not there yet

Towns, industries, water, trees, building and trains. They arrive in blocks 1.2 to 1.5, so
please do not report them as bugs.

## How to report

Open an issue with the label `playtest`, or just tell the agent. Include your phone model and
browser.
