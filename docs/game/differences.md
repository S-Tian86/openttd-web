# Differences from OpenTTD

Everything listed here differs from OpenTTD 15.3 on purpose. Anything not listed must behave
like OpenTTD; a mismatch is a bug.

| Area                      | OpenTTD                                                                  | openttd-web                                                                                                                                                                 | Why                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| View                      | Isometric                                                                | Top-down 3/4                                                                                                                                                                | New look; game logic keeps OpenTTD's grid                                                                                      |
| Graphics, sound, music    | Original sets                                                            | Original art, no audio yet                                                                                                                                                  | Own art style and themes; no third-party assets                                                                                |
| NewGRF                    | Supported                                                                | Not supported                                                                                                                                                               | Out of scope (master plan §2.8)                                                                                                |
| AI and Game Scripts       | NoAI / GameScript                                                        | Not supported                                                                                                                                                               | Out of scope (master plan §2.8)                                                                                                |
| Scenario editor           | Yes                                                                      | No                                                                                                                                                                          | Out of scope (master plan §2.8)                                                                                                |
| Network play              | Yes                                                                      | Not yet                                                                                                                                                                     | Needs a backend (master plan §2.9)                                                                                             |
| World generation          | Same map on every machine                                                | Same seed gives the same world on one browser engine; another engine may differ                                                                                             | TerraGenesis's floating-point steps are ported as in C++ (ruling R3); multiplayer will send the map instead of regenerating it |
| Random numbers            | One stream from the seed                                                 | Same generator and formulas; the sequence of draws differs                                                                                                                  | D1: draws with no effect on a Stage 1 map are not replicated (ruling R1)                                                       |
| Terrain                   | Hills                                                                    | TerraGenesis heights are flattened to sea (0) and land (1); no hills until Stage 2                                                                                          | D4 / ruling R2: relief arrives with Stage 2                                                                                    |
| Rivers                    | Generated                                                                | Not generated until Stage 2                                                                                                                                                 | Springs need higher ground (ruling R1)                                                                                         |
| Lighthouses, transmitters | Generated                                                                | Not generated yet                                                                                                                                                           | Objects are not in Stage 1                                                                                                     |
| Building on slopes        | Allowed with foundations                                                 | Rail, depots and stations need flat land ("Flat land required"); towns and industries as before                                                                             | D4 / R26: Stage 2 adds foundations                                                                                             |
| Town ratings              | Each town rates each company; demolition and stations need it            | Ratings stay at the initial 500 and never change: houses can always be demolished                                                                                           | R15: Stage 2 ports ratings                                                                                                     |
| Town roads                | The bulldozer removes town roads and level crossings (rating permitting) | Town roads cannot be removed by the player's bulldozer (except a one-piece stub under new rail, a depot or a station); bulldozing a crossing removes only the rail          | D2 / R16 / R33: Stage 2 with town ratings                                                                                      |
| Joining stations          | Ctrl builds a separate adjacent station; distant join window             | A new station joins the single adjacent station or is new; no distant join, and Ctrl does not build a separate adjacent station. Reuse of a nearby unused station is ported | R18: simpler tool for Stage 1                                                                                                  |
| Company name              | "{Town} Transport" and a manager face                                    | "Your company"                                                                                                                                                              | R27: company names arrive with company management                                                                              |
| Track fences              | Drawn                                                                    | Stored but not drawn                                                                                                                                                        | R32: art block 1.6                                                                                                             |
| Building by touch         | Mouse only; Shift shows the cost                                         | Finger down/up are press/release; a Confirm button; the ghost and cost bar show while dragging                                                                              | R20, R30, R31; spec §4                                                                                                         |
| Remove toggle             | Ctrl toggles Remove for rail and station tools                           | Ctrl does not toggle Remove; use the Remove button                                                                                                                          | Touch-first tools                                                                                                              |
| Area demolition           | Ctrl gives diagonal area demolition                                      | No diagonal area demolition on touch                                                                                                                                        | Touch-first tools                                                                                                              |
| Depot placement           | Built on press                                                           | Built where the drag is released, so it matches the ghost under the finger                                                                                                  | Touch-first tools                                                                                                              |
| Land Area Information     | Lists tile details                                                       | Also names the station or depot                                                                                                                                             | More useful on touch                                                                                                           |
| Cost while paused         | Shift still shows estimates while paused                                 | The cost bar shows the refusal instead of the estimate                                                                                                                      | Simpler cost bar                                                                                                               |
| Dates                     | Day with ordinal ("1st Jan 1950")                                        | Day without ordinal ("1 Jan 1950")                                                                                                                                          | Simpler formatting                                                                                                             |
| Owner errors              | "... owned by ..." errors                                                | Not shown: there is one company                                                                                                                                             | Stage 2 adds companies                                                                                                         |
| Town growth               | Towns grow during play                                                   | Towns keep their generated size                                                                                                                                             | Ruling R4: growth and ratings come in a later stage                                                                            |
| Industries                | 10 temperate types                                                       | Coal mines and power stations only; picks of other types place nothing                                                                                                      | Ruling R10: the number of mines and power stations matches OpenTTD                                                             |
| No town fits              | Aborts to the menu                                                       | The current world is kept and the new-game window shows an error                                                                                                            | Ruling R12: never lose the session to an unlucky seed or a mostly-sea map                                                      |
| Industry animation        | Tile animations, sounds                                                  | Industry tiles are static; `OnTick_Industry` (counter, sounds) does not run yet                                                                                             | Block 1.5 adds production and the industry tick                                                                                |
| Town signs                | White text; small far out with a black shadow; a city icon               | Same sizes and contents; dark text on a panel box, far out dark text with a light halo; cities marked with ★                                                                | The light themes need the inverted contrast; the city icon is a glyph until real art (block 1.6)                               |
| Dropping a tool           | Esc, or its toolbar button again                                         | Also a right click that does not drag the map                                                                                                                               | A quick way out with a mouse; OpenTTD's right click does nothing there                                                         |
