# Block 1.2 playtest guide

Preview: https://s-tian86.github.io/openttd-web/preview/block-1-2/

Open it on your phone. Everything here is meant to be tried with your fingers.

## What is new

- Generated worlds: sea, lakes and coasts, rough land and rocks, and forests that grow.
- Towns with English names, roads and houses. Some houses start under construction and
  finish over time.
- Coal mines and power stations.
- A new-game window: map size, number of towns, industries, seed and a random seed button.
- A query tool: tap or click a tile to see what it is. Without the tool, long press a tile.
- Town and industry windows (query a house or an industry tile).
- Share a world with `?seed=N&size=WxH`, for example `?seed=1&size=128x64`.

## What to try on the phone

- [ ] Generate a few worlds of different sizes, including a non-square one (for example
      256×64).
- [ ] Find a town and open its window.
- [ ] Find a coal mine and a power station and open their windows.
- [ ] Query water, a coast tile, trees, a road and bare land.
- [ ] Turn the query tool on, then drag and pinch. No window should pop up.
- [ ] Reuse a seed and check that the same world comes back.
- [ ] Switch to Español and back.
- [ ] Watch houses finish building at ×4 or fast forward.

## Not there yet

Building track, stations, trains, cargo and money flows, town growth (blocks 1.3 to 1.5 and
Stage 2), and hills (Stage 2). Please do not report them as bugs. The full list of deliberate
differences is in [differences.md](differences.md).

## How to report

Open an issue with the label `playtest`, or just tell the agent. Include your phone model and
browser.
